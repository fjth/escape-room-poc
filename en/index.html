The story

In 1922, archaeologist Elise Vermeer vanished without a trace while excavating in the Valley of the Kings. Her final journal entry breaks off mid-sentence, beside a sketch of a door that appears on no map.

A century later, that door has been found — and your team gets to go in first. Behind you, the stone grinds shut.

Somewhere in the dark lies the way out. So does something that has been waiting a very, very long time for company.

Questions

When should we arrive?

About 10 minutes before your start time. That gives us time for a proper briefing, and the door closes right on schedule. Arriving late comes off your playing time.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

You can cancel or move your booking free of charge up to 48 hours in advance. After that we charge the full price. Get in touch and we'll find a new slot together.

Is the room accessible?

The tomb is on the ground floor and wheelchair accessible, with an accessible toilet. Using a wheelchair or walker, or have other needs? Let us know in advance and we'll make sure everyone can play.

Can kids play?

Players aged 12 and up can join. Younger children are welcome with an adult. It's tense and atmospheric, but never genuinely scary.

I'm claustrophobic. Can I still play?

Yes. The door is never truly locked — you can always step out, and a game master keeps an eye on you the whole time. The rooms feel like a tomb, but there's plenty of space to move.

Do you sell gift cards?

We do. A trip into the tomb makes a great gift for the puzzle lover in your life. Email or call us to order one.

Can we come as a company or team?

Absolutely. An escape room is a great team outing: you'll have to communicate, collaborate and win together (or be cursed together). For larger groups we'll put together a custom offer.

How do I pay?

You pay online with iDEAL when you book. Card payments on site work too, for extras or gift cards.
